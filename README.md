# SCYTHERS: the set of the film

The 3D map of the facility of SCYTHERS, a drawn short film by acid//states.
Each shot of the film is staged in this map.

Open the map: https://acidstates.github.io/scythers-set/

Press Play to run the film on the map. The red clock shows the time in the world of the film:
one second of the film is one minute for the heroes. The ending of the film is not on the public map.

The eight security cameras of Kade hang in their rooms (labels CCTV 1 to CCTV 8). The monitor wall in his guard
post shows what they see now: press "Kade: his monitor wall".

Visits are counted with GoatCounter (no cookies, no personal data).
